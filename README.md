# Just Spin It! — 轉一下就對了

Can't decide? Spin it! A cute spin-wheel game for every "I don't know, you pick!" moment.

**Live demo:** https://maokuanghsin.github.io/just-spin-it/

## What it does

Three little wheels, in English (default), Traditional Chinese and Japanese:

- **Travel — where to go next:** pick a continent → country → city, then spin. 40 countries, 162 cities, including Japan (27 cities), United States (24), Taiwan (17) and South Korea (9). Each result shows Must-Visit, Must-Buy and Must-Eat highlights.
- **Food — what to eat:** 30 ideas, spin for tonight's dinner.
- **Activity — what to do:** 30 ideas for today or the weekend.

City highlights from Japan, South Korea, Thailand, Malaysia and Taiwan are shown below the wheels.

## How to use

Open the live demo, pick a wheel, spin, and go with it. Spin again as many times as you like. Runs 100% in the browser — nothing is uploaded or saved.

## Tech

Single static HTML file (`index.html`), no backend, no build step.
